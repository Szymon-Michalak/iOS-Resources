Whether you're preparing for system design interviews or building scalable backend systems, this post offers detailed illustrations and explanations with real-world context. 

The author also shares insights from **8+ years of industry experience**, with a focus on making complex topics **approachable and visual** for learners. A highly recommended resource for engineers at all stages looking to strengthen their architecture fundamentals.

#### Key Points
- Breaks down **30 foundational system design topics** with real-world context and diagrams.
- Covers **scaling strategies**, **databases**, **API protocols**, and **networking concepts** clearly.
- Explains **GraphQL vs REST**, **vertical vs horizontal scaling**, and **SQL vs NoSQL** decisions.
- Includes **a companion video**, diagrams, and links to deep-dive articles on each topic.

#### Summary of Contents
- **Client-Server & DNS:** Explains how client requests reach backend servers via IP, DNS, and proxies.
- **HTTP, HTTPS & APIs:** Introduces protocols and formats used in web communication.
- **REST vs GraphQL:** Compares popular API design paradigms with code-level clarity.
- **SQL vs NoSQL Databases:** Differentiates relational vs non-relational storage models.
- **Scaling & Load Balancing:** Discusses vertical/horizontal scaling, sharding, caching, and CDN use.
- **WebSockets & Webhooks:** Covers real-time communication strategies.
- **Microservices & Message Queues:** Describes service decomposition, async messaging, and rate limiting.
- **Distributed Systems & CAP Theorem:** Introduces concepts like consistency, availability, replication, and idempotency.
